AI bots in Europe. They work. You decide.

Chat with Work runs AI bots and an assistant for your team's work, on our servers in Germany with every model in the EU. Bots start on their own: a time of day, a new GitHub issue, an email, a customer's message. They work with the tools you connect, and anything that needs you lands in one queue.

In the app:
• See your bots and what they did while you were away.
• Work through Decisions: approve a change, edit a drafted reply, or answer a question, one at a time, with why you're asked.
• Read your morning briefing, ready before you start.
• Ask anything about your work and get answers with their sources.
• Get a notification when something waits for you.
• Dictate instead of typing: speech is transcribed in the EU.
• Rate an answer, or report one as offensive or harmful, from its menu.

BOTS THAT WORK WHILE YOU'RE AWAY
Start from a recipe or describe the job: triage new issues, chase overdue invoices, answer customers, brief you every morning. A bot follows its instructions, works with the integrations you give it, and stays within its monthly cap.

CHANGES ASK FIRST
Every tool you connect starts at Read. Changes ask first, shown exactly as they'll run, unless you let a bot or a chat run them. Admins decide what the organization allows.

EVERY TOOL YOU USE
Files, mail, chat, projects, CRM, and finance tools, the vendors' own MCP servers, and your own. Connect your tools in the app or on chatwithwork.com.

BUILT FOR TEAMS
Share bots and chats in projects. Each person's connected accounts stay their own: sharing a chat shares the answers, not access to the sources.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The app and its data are hosted in Germany. Your questions, and what the assistant reads to answer them, go to the model you pick: Gemini on Google Vertex AI, GPT on Microsoft Azure, Claude on AWS Bedrock, or Qwen on Hetzner, each in the EU. The app asks before your first question goes to them.
• No index of your company: it searches live when you ask.
• Chats and the text it reads are encrypted at rest.
• We don't train models on your data, and our model providers' terms don't allow it either.
• Web search is off by default. Turned on, its queries go to Google or Bing outside the EU.

Free to start, with 30 answers a day on free models.

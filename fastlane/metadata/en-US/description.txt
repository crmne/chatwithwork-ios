AI bots in Europe. They work. You just decide.

Chat with Work runs AI bots and an assistant for work on our servers in Germany, with every model in the EU. Hand them the job: they search the tools you use, draft and post, and come back on a schedule. Your team shares them, and nothing changes in your tools until you approve it.

The app puts them in your pocket:
• Ask anything about your work and get answers with their sources.
• Approve or deny a change, like a Slack post or a new file, shown exactly as it will run.
• Get a notification when an answer is ready or a change waits for you.
• Read and continue your team's project chats.
• Dictate instead of typing: speech is transcribed on our servers in the EU.
• Attach photos and files, and share answers.

EVERY TOOL YOU USE, IN ONE PLACE
Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Outlook, Teams, Slack, Discord, Basecamp, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Jira, HubSpot, Odoo and many more, plus your own MCP servers. You connect them on chatwithwork.com from a computer, and the app uses them from then on. Every connection starts at Read, so it only reads until you allow changes.

NOTHING CHANGES WITHOUT YOU
Every change to your tools waits for your approval, shown exactly as it will run. Admins can turn changes off for the whole organization.

IT COMES BACK TO YOU
"Every weekday at 8, brief me on the launch." Each run starts a new chat as you, at your connectors' access levels, and you hear when it's ready.

BUILT FOR TEAMS
Projects share chats with the people on them, while each person's connected accounts stay their own: sharing a chat shares the answers, not access to the sources.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The app and its data are hosted in Germany, and the models (Gemini, GPT, Claude, and Qwen) run in the EU.
• No index of your company: it searches live when you ask.
• Chats and the text it reads are encrypted at rest.
• We don't train models on your data.
• Web search is off by default. Turned on, its queries go to Google or Bing outside the EU.
